## 7. Mistakes first-time advertisers make, and how this plan avoids them

| # | Mistake | What it wastes | How the plan avoids it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pressing **Boost** in the Instagram or TikTok app | You lose control of placements, countries, schedule and link | Ads Manager only (2.3, 3.2) |
| 2 | Wrong **time zone or currency** on the ad account | Cannot be changed; a new account starts from zero | USD + Europe/Sarajevo, set once (1.5) |
| 3 | **Cold ads** before any post has proved itself | About $200 per buyer at SaaS benchmarks [plan §2] | Only posts that pass the 48-hour gate (2.1, 3.1) |
| 4 | Optimising for **purchases** with no pixel or events | Learning never ends; Meta wants ~50 events a week [plan §2] | Traffic + landing page views / clicks; judge on `/go/` sales |
| 5 | Treating the **daily budget as a cap** | Meta may spend up to 75% more on a day (finding 2) | Campaign and account spending limits = the card's money |
| 6 | **Editing a live ad** (budget, audience, creative) | Resets learning; the short read becomes noise | No changes during a slot; A03 duplicates instead |
| 7 | Leaving **Advantage+ creative enhancements**, multi-advertiser ads or automatic placements on | AI-altered ads, unapproved text, cheap Audience Network clicks | All off; 4 manual placements (2.3 steps 20, 33, 34) |
| 8 | Chasing **cheap-CPM countries** for "more clicks" | Low-intent clicks, refunds and $15 disputes [plan §4.5] | 16 high-income English-speaking countries |
| 9 | **Stacking narrow interests** | Expensive, starved delivery | Advantage+ audience; interests as suggestions only |
| 10 | **Many ads** on a tiny budget | No ad gets enough data | One proven ad per slot |
| 11 | Trusting the platform's **"results" or ROAS** | Counts organic buyers and view-throughs | `/go/` code → Whop first payment, 7 days after the last click |
| 12 | **Whop store links, or UTMs typed into the URL-parameters box** | Sales cannot be traced to the ad | `/go/` link only; URL parameters empty (1.13) |
| 13 | Boosting content that **cannot run** (licensed music, a caption with "free", no #ad) | Rejected ads, lost days, policy strikes | Gate checks music, caption and disclosure before posting (finding 3) |
| 14 | Spending in **Black Friday week** | Higher CPMs | Blackout Fri 20 Nov to Wed 2 Dec |
| 15 | **Scaling after one sale** | Doubling down on luck | Scale only at CAC ≤ $50 over 3+ net payers, +20% steps, 48-72 h apart |
| 16 | **No stop rule**, or not looking in the first hours | A broken link burns the whole budget | 1-hour check; $10, $30 and $50 stops |
| 17 | **Workarounds after a rejection** (new accounts, a friend's card, another country, VPN) | Permanent bans, including the organic accounts | Section 9; plan §3.5 |
| 18 | Forgetting the **EU beneficiary/payer** | The ad sits unpublished at start time | Defaults set on day 1 (1.7, 3.2 step 16) |

## 9. If an ad is rejected or an account is restricted or disabled

**First, three rules**
1. **Do not create a new ad account, profile or portfolio** to get around it. TikTok says so outright for appeals [F, [TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-troubleshoot-a-suspended-ad-account-on-tiktok)]; on Meta it can be treated as circumvention [unverified].
2. **One appeal per incident.** Multiple appeals delay the review [F, TikTok; S, unverified for Meta].
3. **Tell support only true things.** Never use someone else's account, card, country or a VPN [plan §3.5].

### 9.1 Meta: an ad is rejected

1. Ads Manager → the ad shows **Rejected** → click it → read the policy reason.
2. Usual causes for us: a claim not visible in the ad, landing page not matching the ad, "personal attributes" wording, licensed music, a caption with a banned word.
3. Fix the cause in the ad, or ask the creator for a fixed post and a new code.
4. If you think the rejection is wrong: **Request review** from the ad or from **Business Support Home** (business.facebook.com/business-support-home) [S, unverified: third-party recovery guides found today say Business Support Home has replaced Account Quality, e.g. [Leadsie](https://www.leadsie.com/blog/recover-disabled-facebook-ad-account)].
5. Do not publish the same rejected ad again unchanged.
6. If the fix is not live by the start time, the slot simply starts later. **Never extend the end date** past Thu 19 Nov (A01) or Fri 4 Dec (A02).

### 9.2 Meta: the ad account (or the portfolio) is restricted or disabled

1. **Business Support Home** → select the restricted account → under **What you can do**, click **Request review**. Only admins can request it [S, unverified].
2. Before you submit: pay any unpaid balance; remove or fix any violating ad; make sure two-factor authentication is on; finish business verification (1.9) [S, unverified].
3. Have ready: the d.o.o. documents from 1.9, proof of the domain (1.8), and one paragraph on what Makeborne sells (a $20-99/month software subscription, ads link to makeborne.com).
4. Meta may ask for your ID; give your own, as the real admin [S, unverified].
5. Time window: reviews must be requested within **180 days** [S, unverified]. Reported review times are a few business days [S, unverified].
6. Keep organic posting going. Nothing in the plan depends on paid ads to continue.
7. **If your personal profile is restricted**, the founder (second admin, 1.3) keeps the business running while you appeal through Accounts Centre.

### 9.3 Meta: payment failed, account on hold

1. Billing & payments → see the failed charge → fix the card (1.6 "If the card is declined").
2. **Pay now** for the outstanding balance with the fixed card. Delivery resumes after payment [S, unverified].

### 9.4 TikTok: an ad is rejected

1. Ads Manager → the ad shows the rejection reason → fix it (caption cannot be edited on Spark: you need a different post or a new code).
2. If the rejection is wrong: **? Help → Ad Assistant → Submit ticket → Issue category: Account/Ad Review** → describe → Submit [F, [TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-troubleshoot-a-suspended-ad-account-on-tiktok)].

### 9.5 TikTok: the ad account is suspended

1. Open the **Account Health** tab. "Attention needed" means fix now; "Poor" means suspended [F, [TikTok, Jun 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/account-suspensions?lang=en)].
2. **First violation: you have 30 days** to fix the issues or appeal. After that, or if the appeal fails, the suspension becomes permanent [F].
3. Appeal: **? Help → Ad Assistant → Submit ticket → Account/Ad Review → Account Review → Account Suspension Appeal** → details → **Submit**. Expect a reply within **two business days**. Appeals after **180 days** are not reviewed [F].
4. Do not create a new ad account while the appeal is open [F].
5. A suspended account can request a refund of unused balance through TikTok's separate process [F].

### 9.6 When to stop waiting and use the plan's fallback

| If this is still broken on... | Then [plan] |
|---|---|
| **Mon 9 Nov** (Meta billing) | A01 on TikTok at $51/day × 2 (if TikTok bills); else b5 → creator rehires |
| **Mon 16 Nov 00:00** (Meta account or ad, A01's last start) | A01 does not run in slot A; record it; owner decides at the Mon 16 Nov offer check |
| **Tue 1 Dec** (TikTok account or ad) | A02 on Meta at $26/day × 4, Thu 3 - Sun 6 Dec (3.3) |
| Both platforms unusable | b5 → creator rehires; their posts can be boosted later once an account works |

Write every incident (date, platform, reason, what you did, outcome) in the private notes.
