## 0. The plan on one page

1. **The story:** a Sarajevo company launches an AI tool that sells worldwide and joins the race with Silicon Valley and Stockholm. It is backed by things journalists can test: one brief, a stopwatch, a live site.
2. **Press, in three waves:**
   - Thu 29 Oct 08:30: the **post-launch** release, no embargo, to every BiH outlet at once: the 12 trusted outlets first (list EMB), then the rest (Bosnian, Croatian for Mostar, Serbian ijekavica for Banja Luka). If there was no GO at 01:10, it waits for the owner's new time.
   - Tue 3 Nov 10:00: the regional tech pitch (war room slot), with what happened in week 1.
3. **TV and radio:** pitch morning shows on Thu 29 Oct for 2-6 Nov. The hook is a site built live in the studio: the host gives the brief.
4. **Two anchor stunts:** a press demo where **journalists give the brief** (Fri 30 Oct 10:30), and **"Kazandžiluk online"**, websites for Baščaršija craftsmen in one day (Sat 7 Nov).
5. **No street stunts in the window.** Municipal public-space permits take 20-60 days ([Stari Grad, 20 days](https://starigrad.ba/datoteke/uploads/2025/10/11-ODLUKA-O-IZMJENAMA-I-DOPUNAMA-ODLUKE-O-PRIVREMENOM-KORISTENJU-JAVNIH-POVRSINA-NA-PODRUCJU-OSGS.pdf); [Centar, decision within 30 days](https://www.centar.ba/upload/modules/documents/2026/dac45103cd5b5b26a7814a0e880ceaed.pdf); [Novo Sarajevo, 60 days](https://ereg.novosarajevo.ba/view/98/Izdavanje-odobrenja-za-privremeno-korištenje-javnih-površina-na-području-Općine-Novo-Sarajevo,-te-utvrđivanje-naknade-za-korištenje-iste)). Every stunt runs **indoors, on private premises, with the owner's written yes**.
6. **Money:** 214 KM ($122.42) core, up to 354 KM ($202.50) with options, plus about 45 extra builds from the demo-build line D14 (cost in the owner's private notes). Decisions settled in §8 (owner, 27 Oct). Rate used: **1 USD = 1.748150 KM** ([CBBH, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.cbbh.ba/CurrencyExchange/)).

**The week you are launching into:** BiH held general elections on 4 Oct 2026 and government formation still leads the front pages ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bosnian_general_election); N1, 26 Oct). **Bonus headline (only if it really happens):** **Brže od ćevapa: web stranica za ćevabdžinicu gotova prije nego što je stigao tanjir** / **Faster than ćevapi: a website for a Baščaršija grill was live before the plate arrived.** If the ćevapi win, publish that instead. Never stage the result.

**Words to use and avoid**
- Say "jedan brief" (one brief), or "jedan brief, odnosno pisani opis". Never "kratak" (short). Every real build came from a 500-1,000-character brief, so never call it "jedna rečenica".
- Say "ulazi u utakmicu" (joins the race). Never "bolji od Lovablea" (better than Lovable), "bosanski Lovable" or "klon".
- Say "u našim testnim izradama" (in our test runs) before any build time.
- Say "postoje samo plaćeni paketi" (paid plans only). Never use "besplatno", "probni period", "free" or "trial", even to say Makeborne doesn't have them.
- Never name an AI model or vendor.

## 2. Media list

**How to read it.** "Contact route" is the outlet's own public contact page, plus the generic newsroom address published on it. Several sites hide addresses behind Cloudflare email protection; the addresses below were decoded from those public pages [F]. No private person's email or phone is listed.

**Best day and time (rules of thumb)**
- **Portals:** weekday, 08:00-09:30. N1's news sitemap for Mon 26 Oct 2026 shows nothing before 07:00, 7-10 items an hour at 07:00-09:00 and a peak at 10:00-14:00 [F, proxy]. A release that lands at 08:00 is in the morning queue.
- **Agencies:** send the media advisory (najava) the day before, by 12:00, and the release at once. FENA publishes about 250 items a day and has a "Najave" category ([FENA](https://www.fena.ba/category/39)) [F].
- **Clean releases get copied.** The NetWork 14 organiser's release ran almost verbatim on Klix, Biznisinfo, Tuzlanski and Srpskainfo on 22-26 Oct 2026 [F]. Send ready-to-publish text and photos.
- **TV morning shows:** pitch 3-7 working days ahead and reconfirm the day before [E]. No BiH show publishes a "suggest a topic" page [F]; use the generic desk addresses.
- **TV news crews:** a weekday event at 10:00-12:00 in central Sarajevo gives crews time to cut for news at 18:30-19:30 [E]. Main news: BHT1 19:00, FTV and RTRS 19:30, Nova BH 18:30, Hayat around 18:52 [S, raspored.tv listings, 27 Oct 2026].
- **Invite everyone at once.** BiH and regional journalist associations publicly criticise selective invitations ([021.rs example](https://www.021.rs/info/srbija/314885/uns-srpska-lista-samo-pojedine-medije-zvala-na-konferenciju)) [S].
- **Embargoes:** not used since the launch moved to 01:30 (the release goes out after it). For later news: the BiH press code does not mention embargoes ([VZS kodeks](https://vzs.ba/kodeks-za-stampane-i-online-medije-bih/)) [F]. Don't rely on one. A broken embargo costs little here: the countdown and waitlist are live, so an early story still sends people to the waitlist.

## 4. Guerrilla stunts in Sarajevo (11, ranked by expected press coverage per KM)

### 4.1 Ground rules for every stunt

- **Indoors, on private premises, with the owner's written yes** (`c12`). No tables, screens, banners or crowds on the street.
- **Why:** using public space needs a municipal decision. Stari Grad wants the request at least 20 days ahead ([SN KS 6/25](https://starigrad.ba/datoteke/uploads/2025/10/11-ODLUKA-O-IZMJENAMA-I-DOPUNAMA-ODLUKE-O-PRIVREMENOM-KORISTENJU-JAVNIH-POVRSINA-NA-PODRUCJU-OSGS.pdf)). Centar decides within 30 days ([form KOM-04](https://www.centar.ba/upload/modules/documents/2026/dac45103cd5b5b26a7814a0e880ceaed.pdf)). Novo Sarajevo takes 60 days ([e-register](https://ereg.novosarajevo.ba/view/98/Izdavanje-odobrenja-za-privremeno-korištenje-javnih-površina-na-području-Općine-Novo-Sarajevo,-te-utvrđivanje-naknade-za-korištenje-iste)). Centar banned info tables and promo stands on Ferhadija and on Maršala Tita from Vječna vatra in Dec 2021 ([centar.ba](https://www.centar.ba/vijesti/18986/uvodjenje-reda-u-oblast-koristenja-javnih-povrsina)); whether that ban still applies is [U]. Baščaršija and Ferhadija sit inside the national monument "Historijski urbani krajolik Sarajevo", so heritage rules add scrutiny [F/U].
- **Gatherings:** a free, non-commercial promotional get-together falls under "drugi oblici okupljanja" of the cantonal public-assembly law (Art. 32-33). It needs no police notification unless its size or nature needs security; then it is 5 days ahead ([Zakon o javnom okupljanju KS](https://www.paragraf.ba/propisi/kantona-sarajevo/zakon-o-javnom-okupljanju.html)). That is my reading [U]. Our events are invited, indoors and small.
- **No contests with prizes.** In the Federation, a prize contest or prize game needs Federal Ministry of Finance approval requested at least 30 days ahead, plus 6% of the prize value ([FMF](https://fmf.gov.ba/Content/Read/igre-na-srecu)). So every gift goes to **everyone who comes**, with no selection, no "best", no draw [interpretation, U].
- **Consent:** written consent (`c11`) from every identifiable person and business filmed or given a site. Parents sign for anyone under 18. The new data-protection law ([SG BiH 12/25, AZLP](https://www.azlp.ba/Novi_zakon_i_obavjestenje/Default.aspx?id=4462&langTag=bs-BA)) wants consent that is voluntary, specific, informed and unambiguous [F].
- **Truth:** real timers only. If a build fails, say so on camera and run it again. Never present a pre-built site as live. Never show the countdown designs as output.
- **Gifted sites** are real Makeborne builds, published on a Makeborne address, with content the recipient approved. They stay up for at least 12 months (OD2). The founder first confirms that a gifted site can stay published at no cost to the recipient, and whether a shop's own photos can be added. Generated images are not live, so promise no pictures.
- **Language:** the founder tests a Bosnian brief on Tue 27 Oct (site, deck, book). If Bosnian output is weak, run demos in English and say why.
- **Tracking codes** (founder registers them in the private code register on Wed 28 Oct; until /go/ works, use the long makeborne.com URL with `utm_source=<code>&utm_medium=press|guerrilla|community&utm_campaign=launch-2026-10`): press `press-emb`, `press-bih`, `press-hr`, `press-rs`, `press-reg`; TV and radio `tv-<show>`, `radio-<show>`; stunts `g-launchnight`, `g-pressdemo`, `g-cevapi`, `g-kazandziluk`, `g-tram`, `g-cafe-<name>`; community `uni-etf`, `uni-ssst`, `uni-ius`, `uni-ibu`, `com-gdg`, `com-briefzadobro`. Each goes as a QR code on that event's fact sheet or card.
- **Cost** below = cash + AI build credits at about $1 (1.75 KM) per build [E, strategy D14 "demo builds about $1"]. Expected coverage = my estimate of published items (web articles, TV or radio segments) [E].

**Things that backfire in BiH media**
- **Paying for an article that looks like journalism.** The press code requires paid content to be labelled ([Art. 19, VZS](https://vzs.ba/kodeks-za-stampane-i-online-medije-bih/)). Several portals sell "promo" slots; the budget is allocated anyway. Don't buy.
- **Inviting some outlets and not others.** Invite every outlet on the same day, with the same text.
- **Political or ethnic framing.** No politicians, ministries, parties or "first from Bosnia" patriotism, especially during government formation. Don't tag officials. Send RS outlets the Serbian ijekavica version and Mostar outlets the Croatian one; don't send Bosnian to everyone.
- **War and siege imagery.** No "from the siege to Silicon Valley" lines.
- **Unprovable firsts:** no "prvi u BiH", "prvi u regiji" or "bosanski unicorn".
