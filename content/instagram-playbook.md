### 3.2 Posting windows and the time test

**What the studies say (viewer's local time):**
- Sprout Social, about 2B engagements across 307K profiles, Nov 2025-Feb 2026: Instagram strongest Mon-Thu around midday to evening, **Wednesday 12:00-21:00** best [F, sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-social-media, 31 Mar 2026; confirmed in `research/short-form-video.md` verification row 21].
- Buffer, 9.6M Instagram posts from its State of Social Engagement 2026 data (Jan 2024-Dec 2025): top slots Thu 09:00, Wed 12:00 and Wed 18:00; **evenings 18:00-23:00** strongest on every weekday except Thursday [S, buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram, 2026; two Buffer pages carry different dates].
- Later's older data (2024-Nov 2025) points to 03:00-06:00 instead [S]. The studies disagree because the samples and time zones differ.

**Why your own Insights override them:** these are averages across millions of accounts with other audiences. Reels are mostly shown to non-followers over several days, so the posting hour matters less for Reels than for feed posts; what matters most is that **the founder is free to reply for the first 60 minutes**. Once the account has 100+ followers, Insights → Total followers → "Most active times" shows when *our* followers are on, and that wins.

**Clock (from Sun 1 Nov, when the US leaves summer time):** CET = UK + 1 h = US Eastern + 6 h = US Pacific + 9 h. Before 1 Nov, US Eastern is CET − 5 h.

| Slot | CET | UK | US Eastern (from 1 Nov) | Founder block (strategy §18) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Midday | **13:15** | 12:15 | 07:15 | 13:00-14:00 comments and DMs | Default Reel slot in `data/calendar.json`; Europe at lunch |
| Evening (test) | **20:15** | 19:15 | 14:15 | 20:00-21:00 evening slot | UK/EU evening + US early afternoon, where both studies overlap |
| Carousel weekday | 18:00 | 17:00 | 12:00 | Before the evening block | Thu carousel |
| Carousel weekend | 12:00 | 11:00 | 06:00 | Weekend | Sat carousel |
| Sunday | 19:00 | 18:00 | 13:00 | Sunday review ends | Winner re-cut or Day-N |
| Trial reels | 21:00 | 20:00 | 15:00 | Evening block | Hook tests (cannot be scheduled [S]) |

**The test:** week 1 posts at the calendar times (baseline). In week 2, the Tue 3 Nov and Wed 4 Nov Reels go out at **20:15** instead of 13:15; Mon 2 and Fri 6 Nov stay at 13:15. On Sun 8 Nov compare reach at 24 h, non-follower share and DM keyword replies per 1,000 views. Repeat in week 3 with the slots swapped (Mon/Fri at 20:15). Adopt the winner from week 4; if the gap is under 20%, keep 13:15 (it fits the founder's day better). Record the result in the Sunday note. Schedule feed posts in Meta Business Suite; post Stories and trial reels live.

### 3.3 Trial reels

- **What they are:** a Reel shown to non-followers first, kept off the grid, the Reels tab and followers' feeds; results show after about 24 h; it can be shared to everyone by hand or auto-shared if it performs well in the first 72 hours [S, inro.social and publer.com trial-reel guides, 2026]. Turn it on at the final sharing screen (Advanced settings / "Trial").
- **Eligibility:** guides report a follower minimum for professional accounts (one says 200) [S/U]. With 2 followers the option may not appear yet, and it hardly matters: almost every early view is a non-follower view anyway. Look for it from week 2.
- **How we use them:** only for **hook tests**. Take a Reel from the previous 7 days, keep the body, replace the first 2 seconds and the on-screen hook with one of the hook-bank alternatives (strategy §4.3), and post it as a trial at 21:00. Up to 3 a week (Mon, Wed, Fri). Auto-share **off**; we decide on Sunday.
- **Promote** a trial to the grid only if, at 72 h, it beats the original's 24-h reach by 50%+ and its sends per reach are at least equal. It then replaces that week's Sunday winner slot, not an extra post.
- **Never** use a trial reel to hide a claim we would not post publicly; the same copy rules apply. Followers can still see trials through DMs or the same audio [S].

### 4.1 The caption formula (Reels and carousels)


### 5.2 Step-by-step configuration (ManyChat; option A follows the same logic)

1. **Prerequisites:** Instagram professional account linked to the Makeborne Facebook Page; "Allow access to messages" on (section 1.7); the owner's own ManyChat login with 2FA.

3. **Create 4 automations** (Automation → New → Instagram → "User comments on your post or reel"):

7. **STOP:** keyword `stop` (also `unsubscribe`) → the STOP message → tag `opted-out` → condition on every automation: skip if `opted-out`.
8. **Human hand-off:** any other reply → notify the founder → out-of-hours message only if no human replies within 15 minutes.
9. **Test** with a second, personal Instagram account before going live: comment each keyword, check the DM arrives, the link resolves with its tags, STOP works.
10. **Logging:** every Sunday, export only **counts** per keyword and post (`dm_keyword_received`, tracking.md §3.3). Never export usernames into this repo or the war-room site.

### 5.3 Compliance (Meta's messaging rules)

- **One private reply per comment, within 7 days of the comment.** Further messages only if the person replies, and then within 24 hours of their reply [F, developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/private-replies, read 26 Oct 2026]. So the first DM must stand alone; everything after it is opt-in (UNCUT, a question).
- **Promotional content only inside the 24-hour window** the person opened; message tags are not a route for promotion; automated chats disclose that they are automated [F, developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/policy/policy-overview, read 26 Oct 2026; outreach.json compliance]. Our first line says "automatic reply"; every message offers STOP.
- **No follow-up sequences** outside the window, no "just checking in" messages, no broadcasts to everyone who ever commented.
- **Rate limits:** private replies are reported at 750 per hour per account [S, vendor blogs citing Meta's Graph API]. Far above our needs; if a post explodes, the tool queues.
- **Opt-in and privacy:** commenting the keyword is the person's request for the message. The tool stores their Instagram username and the conversation, which is personal data: mention the DM tool in the privacy notice, accept the tool's data-processing terms, keep exports private, delete opted-out contacts' data on request.
- **Content:** live features only, no vendor names, no "free", the refund line exactly as the live terms say (update all four messages the day D1 changes).

## 7. Weekly review (inside the Sunday ritual, 20 minutes)

Read Insights per post at 7 days (Reels) and 3 days (carousels and stories). Thresholds are starting points [A]; replace them with our own medians after week 2.

| Metric (Insights name) | How to compute | Good | Act when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sends per reach** | Shares ÷ Accounts reached | ≥1.0% (great ≥2%) [A] | Under 0.3%: the post gives no reason to send; rewrite the CTA line |
| **Average watch time** | Avg watch time ÷ length | ≥50% for cuts under 35 s; ≥35% for 60-90 s [A] | Under 30%: the hook or the first 3 s failed; re-cut with 2 new hooks |
| **Skip rate** (where shown) | As reported | Below the account average | Above average two posts running: change frame 0 |
| **Likes per reach** | Likes ÷ reach | Rising week on week | — |
| **Follows per reach** | Follows ÷ reach | ≥0.3% [A] | Under 0.1%: the profile does not convert; check pins and bio |
| **Profile visits → external link taps** | Link taps ÷ profile visits | ≥10% [A] | Under 5%: swap bio variant (section 1.3) on Monday |
| **DM keyword replies per 1,000 views** | Keyword comments + story replies ÷ views × 1,000 | **≥3** (plan KPI, `data/kpis.json`) | Under 3 for 3 posts in a row: fix the CTA (tracking.md §7.4) |
| **DM → `/go/` click** | `/go/ig-*-<keyword>` clicks ÷ DMs sent | ≥40% [A, kpis.json] | Under 25%: the DM is too long or the link is buried |
| **DM conversations → paid (30 days)** | Paid with first touch `utm_medium=dm` ÷ DMs | ≥3% [A, kpis.json] | Read every conversation that asked a question and didn't buy |
| **`/go/ig-*` sales** | Whop reconciliation (tracking.md §4.3) | ≥1 net payer per 10,000 views across 3+ posts = a house format (strategy §8) | — |

**Recycling winners** (a winner = top 10% by `/go/` clicks plus tracked sales, or 3x the account's median views in 48 h):
1. **Re-cut** with 2 new hooks (strategy §18): one goes in the next Sunday winner slot, one as a trial reel.
2. **Carousel it:** turn the winning build into a 6-slide carousel (brief, 4 frames, CTA).
3. **Story it:** reshare to Stories with a poll, and add it to the matching highlight.
4. **Re-post** the same idea with a new brief and output after 7+ days ("episode 2"), never the same file.
5. **Pin it** if it beats a pinned post on sales.
6. **Boost candidate:** if it meets the plan's gate (3x median views in 48 h, 65%+ 3-second hold, and 1+ tracked sale or 5+ link clicks per 1,000 views), it goes to `content/ads-playbook.md`.

Retire the bottom 50% of formats each Sunday (strategy §18). Never delete a post to clean the grid.

## 9. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Post originals, re-exported from the project file, with Instagram-native covers and captions | Repost TikTok downloads, anything with another app's watermark or end card, or other people's content |
| Show the product working in frame 0, with the real time or "sped up" | Fake timers, staged results, or a build that needed hidden edits |
| Say "coming soon", no date, for images, top-ups, domains, sync, agency tools, lead research | Promote, tease with dates, or show unreleased features |
| Use 3-4 specific hashtags and a keyword-rich first line | Hashtag walls (more than 5 fails anyway), keyword stuffing |
| Reply to every comment in the first hour, publicly and kindly | Delete criticism, argue, or leave objections unanswered |
| Use one automated DM per keyword comment, disclose automation, offer STOP | Promotional follow-ups outside the 24-hour window, message tags for promotion, DM sequences nobody asked for |
| Use `/go/` links only, one code per post per surface | Whop store, Whop affiliate or Whop tracking links; Linktree; personal data in URLs |
| Disclose every paid or affiliated creator post (#ad first, "ad" in the first 3 s, Paid partnership label) | Accept a collab or tag that hides a payment or free product |
| Use fictional, trademark-checked businesses, or real ones only with written consent, public information only, unpublished by default | Build a real business's site uninvited, or show any client or buyer data |
| Grow with comments and value | Buy followers, likes or views; join engagement pods; follow/unfollow; use comment bots; cold-DM pitches |
| Keep the refund line identical to the live terms | Say "free", "trial", "start free", "unlimited", "print-ready" or "better than [competitor]"; name AI model vendors |
| Run one brand account | Open extra faceless, niche or language accounts before day 60 (D16) |
