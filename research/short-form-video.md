Research track: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn video for AI tools and SaaS, 2025-2026, applied to Makeborne.
Written 26 October 2026, the day the Makeborne paid beta launches. Web pages were read on 26 October 2026 unless another date is given.

## 0. Summary: the answer in 15 points

1. **For a $20-$99/month tool with no free tier, short-form sells proof, not vibes.** Entertainment-first AI content can win huge reach and still fail to convert.
   - Cluely's founder says one UGC video reached 49M views and produced fewer than 100 downloads [V, self-reported].
   - Only 3 of Cluely's 61 visible TikTok captions mention a product feature [F, public counts read 1 Oct 2026].
   - Makeborne's core format must therefore show **one brief becoming a finished website, book or deck, on screen, at honest speed**.
2. **The companies Makeborne is chasing grew mostly on organic social, creators and loops, not paid ads.**
   - Base44's founder: building in public on LinkedIn worked. Influencer marketing and paid ads did not ("a few thousand dollars with no real return"). Extra credits for users who shared what they built created a viral loop [F, self-reported, interview of 4 Aug 2025].
   - Lovable's head of growth: "Organic today is social. Full stop." Paid performance "isn't a meaningful lever" for a new category [F, 19 Dec 2025].
   - Lovable runs creator-led short-form at scale. Its own Whop Content Rewards clipping campaign paid **$1 per 1,000 views**, capped at $500 per platform per video. The **$26,119** budget is fully spent, across **1,717 creators** and **16.7M approved views** [F, campaign page read 26 Oct 2026].
3. **The single best Makeborne format is the comment-reply speedrun:** "Comment your business and I'll build its website."
   - The viewer's comment becomes the next video, so each video earns comments, a series and a named stakeholder who shares it.
   - It proves the product live and works in a reply-to-comment video on TikTok and a Reel on Instagram.
   - Use it only with the commenter's consent and public information (see section 15).
4. **The hook is the output, not the prompt.**
   - TikTok's own guidance: put the proposition in the first 3 s and the hook in the first 6 s, with on-screen text at 5-10 words per second [F, TikTok Ads Help, updated June 2025].
   - Over 63% of TikTok's highest-CTR ads showed the key message or product in the first 3 s [F, 2020, older data].
   - For Makeborne, frame 0 should be the **finished site, book spread or deck**, with a one-line text hook. The build can then rewind to the brief.
5. **Make every idea in two lengths.** Socialinsider's H1 2026 study covered 8.0M TikToks and 9.8M Reels.
   - **TikTok:** videos under 30 s had the highest engagement rate (6.0%), but 120-180 s videos had about 10x the average views (12,000 vs 1,200) [F].
   - **Reels:** 45-60 s was best for both engagement and views [F].
   - **LinkedIn:** 120-180 s was best for both [F].
   - So make a **25-35 s punch cut** and a **60-150 s full build** of each idea.
6. **Volume buys lottery tickets.** Buffer studied 11.4M TikToks from 150K accounts [F, 8 Oct 2025]:
   - Median views per post stay about flat at any posting rate.
   - The 90th-percentile post grows from 3,722 views (1 post a week) to 14,401 (11+ a week).
   - Plan **10-14 TikToks a week on the brand account** for the first 30 days, with the same masters re-cut natively for Reels and Shorts.
7. **Instagram ranks on watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach.** Instagram's head named these three signals in early 2025. He said sends matter slightly more for reaching non-followers [S: the Sept 2026 summary cites no primary post or video. Re-checked 26 Oct 2026].
   - **YouTube Shorts** counts every play and replay as a view since 31 Mar 2025 [F]. Seamless loops therefore pay off.
   - **TikTok** publishes its signals but not their weights. Completion, rewatches and shares are the most consistently cited [S].
8. **Start with 6-8 genuinely run accounts, not a 12-account farm.**
   - Brand accounts: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn.
   - The founder's own accounts: LinkedIn plus TikTok or Instagram.
   - Up to two niche faceless TikTok accounts, "Made with Makeborne" and one for freelancers and agencies.
   - TikTok's guidelines bar inauthentic behaviour and deceptive multiple accounts. Content with "unoriginal or reused material without anything new" is not eligible for the For You feed [F, TikTok Community Guidelines, Integrity and Authenticity, re-read 26 Oct 2026]. Do not duplicate uploads across accounts.
9. **Links: Instagram is the easy conversion surface, TikTok the hard one.**
   - **Instagram** allows up to 5 bio links with no follower minimum [S]. Comment-keyword DM automation through ManyChat works there.
   - **TikTok:**
     - A clickable bio link needs 1,000 followers or a business account. Under 1,000 followers, some countries also require proof of business registration [F, Buffer, 9 Dec 2025].
     - ManyChat's TikTok channel is DM-trigger only, with no comment triggers [F, Jan 2025; still true in a June 2026 forum answer, S].
     - ManyChat's TikTok channel excludes EU, UK and US-based business accounts. Bosnia and Herzegovina is outside the EU, so check eligibility [F/I].
   - **Plan:** TikTok drives profile visits and search; Instagram DMs and bio links convert.
10. **Measure without pixels.** Give each channel, creator and video a first-party short link such as `makeborne.com/go/tt-0412`, which redirects with UTMs. Then reconcile against Whop sales.
    - Meta, TikTok and Google pixels and retargeting need a consent banner first (EU law). Until then, boosts can optimise for views, profile visits or traffic, not purchases.
11. **Run creator programs in two stages.**
    - **Stage 1 (now, about $400-$600):** 8-12 hand-picked micro-creators. Pay a $30-$50 flat fee per video plus a capped $1.50-per-1,000-views bonus, and give each creator a tracked link. The goal is to learn which angles convert.
    - **Stage 2 (month 2, funded by revenue, timed to the "images are live" beat):** a Whop Content Rewards UGC campaign.
      - The **$1,000 minimum budget** is disputed: ClipSpeed (13 Sep 2026) says $1,000 for every campaign type; FindClout (26 Aug 2026) says none is published; Whop's own docs state no minimum [S, conflicting]. Confirm in the Whop dashboard before planning around it.
      - The **~10% fee** is, per ClipSpeed, **deducted from creator CPM payouts, not added to the brand's bill**; FindClout adds about 2.7% + $0.30 card processing [S]. Budget about 3% on top, not 10%, until Whop confirms.
      - Lovable's campaign worked out at about $1.56 per 1,000 approved views [A: $26,119 / 16.7M]. That is above its stated $1 rate, which the page does not explain (fees, unpaid views or different counting) [I].
12. **Lead with books for the holidays: they are Makeborne's emotional, shareable, Lovable-proof wedge.** Examples: "I turned my grandma's recipes into a real book in 2 minutes" and "a bedtime book starring my son."
    - Sends (shares) are Instagram's strongest signal for reaching non-followers, and gifts make people send.
    - The season runs now to 24 Dec, with Black Friday on 27 Nov 2026.
13. **Serial founder content is the trust engine.** Run "Day N: launching an AI builder from Sarajevo with $1,000" on LinkedIn, TikTok and Reels, showing real Whop numbers.
    - Base44's founder grew this way on LinkedIn.
    - Cluely's numbered TikTok episodes had a **median of 552K views** [F, public counts].
14. **Boost only proven winners.** Spend money on organic posts that beat the account median by at least 3x in 48 hours.
    - TikTok's own guidance is 3-5 meaningfully different creatives per ad group [F].
    - Vendor benchmarks put B2B and SaaS Spark Ads CPMs at roughly $11-$15 [S, low confidence].
15. **Be honest about the maths.** $1M ARR is about 2,300 paying subscribers at a blended $36.60 a month [A].
    - At plausible short-form funnel rates, that takes roughly **4M to 75M qualified views** in 3 months (section 12).
    - $1,000 buys at most about 0.5-1M creator views. **The plan only reaches the goal if organic breakouts and the share loop carry it.**
    - The $1,000 is for discovering the funnel rates. Revenue then funds the CPM programs while cost per paid customer stays below one month of ARPU.

## 2. Platform mechanics in 2025-2026

### 2.1 Ranking signals

| Platform | Confirmed signals | Practical rule for Makeborne |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | **Watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach.** Likes count slightly more with followers, sends slightly more with non-followers. Instagram's head first set this out in Jan 2025. A Sept 2026 summary says it was reaffirmed through 2026 [F/S]. The "3-5x a like" weighting for sends is **not** an official figure [S]. 2026 originality guidance cuts recommendations for accounts that mostly repost [S] | Design for **sends**: "send this to the friend who still has no website", or gift-book ideas. Post originals only, with no TikTok watermark. Use **Trial Reels**, which show to non-followers first and report results after about 24 h, to test hooks [S] |
| **TikTok** | The official explainer (2020) groups interactions, video information and device or account settings. **Weights are unpublished**, and follower count is not a direct factor [S]. Third-party guides agree completion, watch time, rewatches, shares, saves and comments matter most [S] | Win the first 3 s, keep it tight for completion, end on a loop or cliffhanger, and ask for a comment that becomes the next video |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Since **31 Mar 2025**, every start or replay counts as a view. "Engaged views" keeps the old meaning and drives monetisation [F, TechCrunch, 26 Mar 2025] | Make the last frame match the first so loops look seamless. Judge performance by engaged views and the "viewed vs swiped away" rate, not raw views |
| **LinkedIn** | LinkedIn counts a view after 2 s with 50% of the video on screen [S]. A 9:16 vertical-video lane above the feed is reported for 2025, for videos under 3 minutes [V, Sendspark; not re-verified]. Outbound links in a post are widely believed to reduce reach [V] | Upload native 9:16, put the link in the first comment, and lead with an insight, not a logo |

## 4. Hooks for the first 1-2 seconds

**Rules from the evidence:**
1. **Frame 0 is the result**: a finished site, a book spread or a deck slide. Use the proposition by 3 s and the hook by 6 s [F, TikTok, June 2025]. Over 63% of top-CTR TikTok ads showed the key message in 3 s [F, 2020].
2. **Text on screen at frame 0**, 4-8 words, inside the safe zone, at 5-10 words per second [F, TikTok].
3. **Talk to the viewer.** One in three top-VTR TikTok ads break the fourth wall [F, 2020].
4. **Use a number or a clock.** A visible timer makes the claim checkable and holds attention [I].
5. **No logo intro and no fade from black.** LinkedIn's B2B ads study advises leading with an insight or question in the first 4-6 s, not branding [F, Jul 2025].
6. **Write the hook after filming** and test 3 hooks on the same body [V, Riley Brown; FORKOFF].
7. **Retention benchmark to aim for:** keep at least 70% of viewers through 3 s [V, vendor rule of thumb, not a platform number].

**Hook bank.** Customer-facing copy. It names no AI vendors, and every claim must be true on the day it is posted.

## 5. Captions, on-screen text, sound and pictures

**Captions.** Always burn them in, or turn on auto-captions and correct them.
- About 80% of LinkedIn videos are watched without audio, and bulleted captions gave 30% longer dwell [F, LinkedIn Creative Labs, B2B ads, Jul 2025].
- Meta's internal tests found captioned video ads raised view time 12% on average [S, older].
- The higher "85% sound-off" figures trace to vendors [S].

**On-screen text:**
- One idea per card, 3-7 words, high contrast, in the centre safe zone.
- Instagram's bottom UI area grew in late 2025 [V], so keep text above the lower 20% and away from the right-hand icon column.

**Sound:**
- TikTok is sound-on. Fast tracks above 120 BPM often lift view-through [F, 2020].
- **Business accounts on TikTok must use the Commercial Music Library** for all commercial activity, including organic posts, duets and stitches: "Businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial usage" [F, TikTok Ads Help, "Commercial Music Library", updated July 2025, re-read 26 Oct 2026]. Trending chart songs are mostly off-limits to the brand account.
- Use CML tracks, original voice-over or the Makeborne sonic logo.
- Founders and creators on personal accounts can use trending sounds where licensing allows.
- **Voice-over plus captions converts better than music alone** for tutorials and speedruns [I].

**Pictures (carousels, thumbnails and statics):**
- **Carousels:** use 5-6 slides [V, ttcalculator]. Slide 1 is the hook, slides 2-5 are real outputs (a phone mockup of a site, a book spread, a deck slide), and slide 6 is the CTA.
- **Reel covers:** a 9:16 cover with the hook text centred so it survives the profile-grid crop. The grid aspect is not re-verified in this pass.
- **LinkedIn:** document carousels (a PDF deck) suit the freelancer and agency angle. The deck can itself be made in Makeborne, which proves the product [I].
- **Visual direction:** follow `research/creative-assets.md`. Use real output, the warm canvas, and avoid the generic AI look.

## 15. Compliance and honesty rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Disclose every paid or incentivised post.** Use the platform's paid-partnership or branded-content toggle **plus** a clear "#ad" or "paid partnership" at the start of the caption **and** in the video.
   - Credits given for sharing are a material connection, so "I got credits for posting this" counts as disclosure [S, FTC guidance as summarised by several legal blogs; FTC "Disclosures 101" not re-read in this pass].
   - EU consumer law also requires commercial intent to be clear [I].
   - The Jenni AI case shows the reputational cost of skipping this [F].
2. **Never present features that aren't live.** Speed claims are timed and labelled. Concept frames say "AI-generated".
3. **Never say "free" or "trial"** while there is none. The refund line must be exact.
4. **Real businesses and people:**
   - Build a commenter's site only on request.
   - Use public information only.
   - Never publish their site live without written consent.
   - Never show their personal data or customers.
   - Don't imply endorsement.
5. **No fake engagement:** no bought views, likes, followers or accounts, and no engagement pods.
6. **No AI model vendor names** in any customer-facing copy, caption or voice-over.
7. **Tracking:** no pixels or retargeting until the consent banner is live.

## 16. Risks

- **Views without buyers.** Entertainment reach doesn't convert for a paid-only tool (Cluely: 49M views, fewer than 100 downloads [V]). Mitigation: product-proof formats, format-matched landing pages, and tracking per video.
- **The paywall before the "wow"** cuts short-form conversion. Mitigation: the owner's decision on a capped preview (7.2) or a refund-led CTA.
- **Small numbers at the start.** New brand accounts average hundreds of views [F]. Breakouts are probabilistic, so plan for 4 weeks of volume before judging.
- **TikTok link friction:** the 1,000-follower or business-registration rule, and no comment-to-DM. Instagram and YouTube carry the clicks early.
- **Creator fraud and low-quality geo traffic** on CPM programs (views clustering near caps, VPN bypass [V]). Mitigation: caps, minimum views, geo rules, audience screenshots and paying on sales.
- **The Whop Content Rewards minimum (possibly $1,000, disputed)** may not fit month 1. The ~10% fee is reportedly taken from creators' payouts, not added to the brand's cost [S]. Mitigation: Stage 1 direct creators.
- **Positioning conflict.** "I replaced my agency" content can alienate agencies, who buy Studio and Scale. Keep it off the freelancer and agency accounts.
- **Over-claiming during launch beats.** Showing image features before they ship is a trust and consumer-law risk.
- **Platform policy drift.** Recommendation rules, DM-automation support, link rules and music licensing change often. Re-check monthly.
- **Founder bandwidth.** The plan needs about 2-3 hours a day of filming, editing and replying. Batch-film on two days a week, and hire an editor from revenue (Riley Brown uses outside agencies [V]).
- **Attribution gaps without pixels.** Short links and codes miss view-through conversions, so expect under-attribution of about 20-50% [A]. Track "how did you hear about us?" at checkout or onboarding.
